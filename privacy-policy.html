Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026

Privacy at a glance

This app is a self-contained, client-side music theory practice tool. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Information we collect

We do not request an account and do not collect names, email addresses, scores, answers, or other personal information. Test progress exists only in the current browser session and is not sent to a server.

How the app works

The quiz, question bank, and notation are processed in your browser. Opening the app loads its static files; answering questions does not submit data anywhere.

Cookies and third-party services

The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers, or embedded third-party services for collecting user data. Your browser or network provider may maintain ordinary technical logs outside this app’s control.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new revision date. Continued use after an update means you are viewing the revised policy.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, please contact the app owner, HuyNQ.
